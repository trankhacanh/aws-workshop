## Feedback After the Internship

After more than two months of participating in the internship program at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.**, I consider this to be a highly valuable experience for both my academic development and future career orientation. It was also my first opportunity to work with cloud computing services, allowing me to gain practical knowledge beyond what I had learned at university.

Throughout the internship, my mentor always encouraged me to research and explore solutions independently before providing guidance. Whenever I encountered difficulties, instead of receiving the answer immediately, I was often directed to the official AWS documentation or given suggestions on how to approach the problem. At first, this was quite challenging because I had to study a large amount of technical documentation written in English. However, over time, I realized that this learning approach was extremely effective. It helped me develop the habit of analyzing problems carefully and finding solutions on my own before seeking assistance.

In addition to technical knowledge, I also improved many essential soft skills, including time management, progress reporting, teamwork, and communication with my mentor. While working on the project, I realized that communication and the ability to present ideas clearly are just as important as programming skills. Regular discussions with my teammates made the integration of different project modules much smoother and significantly reduced development issues.

After completing the internship, I believe that the learning roadmap, training materials, and mentor support were well organized and suitable for interns. Therefore, at this moment, I do not have any specific suggestions for improvement. I hope the program will continue to maintain its quality and provide more students with the opportunity to experience a professional working environment like I did.

If given the opportunity, I would confidently recommend this internship program to students who are interested in Cloud Computing or Backend Development. I believe it provides an excellent environment for learning modern technologies, gaining practical experience, and building a strong foundation for a future career in software engineering.
